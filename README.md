**Stunning Visuals:** The cinematography and food styling are darkly beautiful and artistic, its something you would'nt expected from a 2013
**Psychological Mind Games:** The intense, complex dynamic between Will and Hannibal keeps you on edge.
**Brilliant Acting:** Mads Mikkelsen and Hugh Dancy give masterclass performances.
**Props** The bodies and killings especially the autopsies are made with greate detail that it really replicates a real dead body.
